Workflow — Brand Identity and Digital Logo System
Purpose

This workflow helps Lynx Visual Division create, refine, redesign, and implement brand identity systems for digital products, websites, landing pages, catalogs, portfolios, and social media.

Use this workflow for:

new brand identity
brand refresh
logo redesign
logo refinement
digital logo adaptation
favicon-first logo systems
social avatar logo versions
website logo implementation
visual identity direction
color and typography systems
mini brand guidelines
client DESIGN.md files
brand + website bundles

The goal is not only to create a nice logo.

The goal is to create a visual identity that works across real digital environments:

website header
favicon
app icon
social media avatar
dark mode
light mode
landing pages
catalogs
PDFs
proposals
presentations
mobile screens
Core Principle

A logo is not the full brand.

A brand identity is the relationship between:

positioning
audience
personality
visual language
typography
color
symbol
layout
tone
consistency
usability
digital scalability

The logo must support the brand, not replace strategic thinking.

Does the brand need social media assets?
Does the brand need a favicon/app icon?

Final Rule

A brand identity is not finished when the logo looks attractive.

It is finished when the identity can be recognized, used, scaled, explained, and implemented consistently across the client’s real digital environments.
