# Nido Canino - Asset Checklist

Purpose: practical asset list for the human designer/content creator. These assets should support the refined direction without replacing the current logo or approved copy.

## Highest Priority Assets

### Hero Photo or Video

Need:

- 1 strong homepage hero photo.
- Optional 5-10 second ambient video loop.

Direction:

- Calm dog in a home-like structured care environment.
- Human presence can appear through hands, leash, gesture, feeding prep, or calm interaction.
- Avoid chaotic group play, cages, clinical tables, over-dark images, and generic stock photos.

Specs:

- Horizontal: 16:10 and 4:3 crops.
- Mobile vertical crop: 4:5 or 3:4.
- Export WebP/JPEG optimized.
- Keep original high-res source.

## House and Space Photos

### Rest Area

Show:

- calm bed, blanket, quiet corner, relaxed dog if possible.
- clean but real environment.

Avoid:

- messy clutter,
- empty corner with no emotional cue,
- harsh overhead light.

### Feeding Area

Show:

- bowl, food routine, water, leash/harness nearby, orderly setup.
- optional human hand preparing food.

Avoid:

- chaotic kitchen,
- open food bags as main focus,
- unclean surfaces.

### Guided Play / Coexistence Area

Show:

- small controlled interaction,
- toy or enrichment activity,
- dog observing or calmly participating.

Avoid:

- overexcited group pile,
- too many dogs,
- rough play as the main message.

### Observation / Adaptation Moment

Show:

- human calmly supporting dog,
- leash/harness guidance,
- dog looking at caregiver,
- slow introduction or quiet interaction.

This is important for emotional attachment needs and sensitive dogs.

## Specialized Care Assets

### Senior Dog Care

Show:

- older dog resting, slow walk, ramp/soft surface, careful handling.
- warmth and dignity.

### Mobility Needs

Show:

- non-clinical assistance,
- controlled movement,
- supportive environment.

### Sensitive / Attachment Needs

Show:

- close but calm human-dog connection,
- observation,
- routine,
- emotional safety.

Avoid dramatic sadness. The tone should be competent and reassuring.

## Outdoor / Nearby Park Assets

Need:

- 3-5 photos of nearby outdoor walking context.
- Include leash-controlled calm walks.
- Show neighborhood/park only if it builds trust.

Avoid:

- off-leash chaos,
- crowded park scenes,
- images that imply uncontrolled socialization.

## Gallery Assets

Need:

- 2-4 curated photos per dog.
- A mix of portraits, rest, interaction, routine, and gentle play.
- Clear dog names and permission status.

Metadata to capture:

- Dog name.
- Moment/category: descanso, convivencia, adaptación, paseo, rutina, espacio.
- Service context.
- Photo date/month if useful.
- Permission for website/social.
- Suggested crop focus.

## Video Placeholders

Future slots:

- Homepage hero ambient care.
- Space walkthrough.
- Guided calm interaction.
- Feeding/rest routine.
- Outdoor controlled walk.
- Short gallery moments.

Rules:

- Use muted autoplay only if performance remains strong.
- Always provide poster image.
- Keep duration short.
- Avoid abrupt cuts and high-energy music.

## Social Media Icons

Need:

- Instagram.
- WhatsApp.
- Google/login symbol.
- Optional TikTok/Facebook only if active and relevant.

Direction:

- Use simple line icons or official brand icons with restraint.
- Ensure visible on dark and warm light backgrounds.

## Asset Quality Checklist

Before using an asset:

- Is it real and trustworthy?
- Does it communicate calm, structure, care, and compatibility?
- Does it avoid mass daycare signals?
- Does it work on mobile crop?
- Is the dog/person permission clear?
- Is the file optimized?
- Is there alt text?
- Does it fit the warm camel/dark/cream palette?
